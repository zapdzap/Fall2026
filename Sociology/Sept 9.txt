###
Theoretical Frameworks
###
1. Social Conflict -> Society is a system of inequalities that generate conflict and change.
2. Structural Functionalism -> Society is a system of interrelated parts that work together to maintain stability and social order.
3. Symbolic Interactionism -> Society is an ongoing process of meaning-making through social interaction.
