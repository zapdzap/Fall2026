Culture: The sum of the social categories and concepts we embrace in addition to beliefs, behaviors and practices; everything but the natural environment around us.

Material Culture: Constructed, physical environment, technology, etc.

Nonmaterial culture: Values, beliefs, behaviors and social norms

Ethnocentrisms: The belief that one's own culture or group is superior to others, and the tendency to view all other cultures from the perspective of ones own.
