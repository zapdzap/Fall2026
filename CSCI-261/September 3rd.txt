Inductive Definition -- like recursion

- Inductive reasoning can aslso be applied to many definitions, especiially in computer sicence.
- Commonly this is used to generate or construct sets or objects from some initials building blocks.
- Once some initial elements are included the set, then the remaining items can be constructed from the previous ones by some set of iterative rules
    - These rules are self-referential, and naturally leads to self-similar structure

- we call this an inductivedefinitions (sometimes referred to as a recursive definition)
- This is not unlike functional recursion from the bottom up
- for example the merge operation in merge sort is defined inductively as follows:
    - The merge of two empty lists is the empty list
    - The merge of a non-empty list and an empty list is the non-empty list
    - The merge of two non-empty lists is the list whose first element is the smaller of the first elements of the two lists, followed by the merge of the rest of the two lists.

-Note that inductive definitions are constructive and frequently, but not always, construct infinite sets.
