Permissions Challenges
    - when programs are executed the coe has permissions (via executing user, setuid, setgid, etc.)
    - Many programs need elevated (root) permissions to functions
        -Web servers & listen and respond on http, port 80
        - Email Servers listen & respond on SMTP, e.g port 25,
        - Mobile apps: listening for text mesaages vs. displaying previews.
        - What Permissions are truly needed, and when?

    - Some programs have entirely seperate functions that should not impact each other
        - Browsers: one web page should not impact the others.
    - Solutions: design your architecture around your permission needs.


Distrustful Decomposition
    - Decompose the system into seperate processes with seperate permissions
