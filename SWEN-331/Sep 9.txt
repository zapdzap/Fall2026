Misuse vs. Abuse

Misue is unintentional
Abuse is intentional.


E.g. Maintain Drug interactions

-Actor: Hospital Administrator
-Precondition: Admin is authenticated.
-Main flow:
    1. Admin selects different drug codes from the national drug codes selection menu
    2. Admin enters a minimum dosage for both drugs
    3. Admin enters text notes about the potnential consequences of the interaction.
    4. Admin is shown a table of the patient records where the interaction rule would apply.
    5. Admin saves the interaction rule.
